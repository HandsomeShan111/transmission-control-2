# Visual check

Every screen and state of the game, captured at four sizes, compared with the design's art direction, screen layouts and the designer's previews. Sizes: phone portrait 360 by 640, phone landscape 640 by 360, tablet 768 by 1024, desktop 1920 by 1080. Chromium throughout, Firefox spot-checked. The screenshots are in the Visual check album: each fix shows before and after.

## First impression

The game is the designed console: the right palette, frame proportions, teletype paper and flat shapes, at every size. Frame sizes match the design to the pixel at every size measured. No colour outside the palette, no text below its contrast, no sideways scroll, no control under touch size on the phone. The weak points were fine detail, now fixed.

## Fixed

- Printouts: text ran into the paper's right perforations. Now 32 columns sit between them, as the design sets.
- Picture on screen: the picture hid the "PICTURE ON SCREEN. SAVE BY HAND." line. Now the last three lines stay in view below it.
- RUN and AUDIO switches: the toggle was a small dark blob, up and down hard to tell apart. Now an orange lever, as the designer's preview draws it, leaning clearly up or down.
- Stopped items: several items stopped at one jack piled on each other and on the jack label. Now they fan out across the gap, each readable.
- Arriving items: an item at a destination's input was drawn under the jack. Now above it.
- Destination labels: the label of an extra result row sat under its sign. Now beside it.
- Tablet: after a run, the view cut the first destination's input jack in half. Now whole.
- Shared picture: the difference ring was larger than on the board, and the link text crowded the QR code. Now the board's size, and a clear margin round the code so phone cameras read it.
- Long printouts: the last row showed cut in half under the dashed line. Now only whole rows show.
- Agent faces: small light arcs showed at the band's corners. Gone.
- Held wire: the blinking rings covered the first letter of agent rules. Now clear.
- Save code field: it held 30 columns, not 32, and broke words. Now 32, wrapping at spaces.
- Standby: the blinking ring round the RUN plate was clipped on the phone. Now a ring round the whole plate at every size.
- Lamps: a lit lamp's ring touched its label. Now spaced.
- Agent faces on desktop: the check limit label ("MAX 2 CHECKS PER ITEM") was too small to read. Now the size of the agent's rule.

## Final round, after your changes

Every screen captured again at the four sizes, on the final code. Looked at hardest: the title, the strip above the board, the keys, MEDALS and COMMENDATIONS, NEXT on a report, and order 30 zoomed out. The screenshots are in the Final screens album.

- Keys: one size at each screen size, each in the same place on the title, board, order list, archive and COMMENDATIONS. Measured to the pixel.
- Key sizes: 67 by 48 on a phone, 90 by 62 on a tablet, 108 by 72 on desktop.
- Phone: keys in two fixed rows, upright and sideways. Desktop and tablet: one line.
- The strip: the order's name, lamps and description fit at every size, with no text cut.
- Zoom out: order 30, the largest board, fits whole at every size.
- Nothing new to fix.

## Kept as the design draws them

You may want to change these at Test; each follows the design as written.

- The master alarm lamp, when off, is an empty ring above the destinations with no label.
- A report paper is centred on the window, so on desktop it covers the result rows it reports on.
- A held wire starts at the jack's centre and covers its label.

## Decisions where the design is silent

- Several items stopped at one jack fan across the flow, 8 apart, in the order they stop.
- The switch lever is a quarter of the plate wide and leans 32 degrees up or down.
- The standby ring round the square RUN plate is a rounded square.
- The check limit label on faces is the size of the agent's rule.
- The QR code keeps a margin of four modules.

## Not checked here
