# Your first shift

This tutorial takes you from the title screen through order 1 and into order 2. It takes about five minutes. You need a phone or a computer with a browser. Sound helps but is not needed.

## 1. Open the game

Open https://handsomeshan111.github.io/transmission-control-2/.

On the first visit, LOADING lines print while the game keeps its files. Then the title shows: TRANSMISSION CONTROL 2, with START under it.

Press START. The board opens on the night staff's wiring, and the RUN key blinks.

## 2. Watch the misfire

Tap anywhere on the board. The night staff's wiring runs: items leave the INPUT tray and travel along the wires. Something arrives wrong, the red lamp lights and the alarm sounds.

The teletype prints an incident report. It ends "FILED: 1 of 3." The night staff found one of three hand-written results of this order. You can find the other two later, on purpose.

Tap the report to close it. A memo from the Department of Records prints: your first act. Tap it to close it. Order 1 opens.

## 3. Read the order

The strip above the board names the order, 1 Signing ceremony, and says what it needs: the treaty, the address and the star chart, laminated, at the World Council.

On the board:

- INPUT, on the left, holds the batch: the items this order sends.
- LAMINATOR, in the middle, is an agent. Its rule: "Laminates it."
- World Council, on the right, is the destination. Its rows show what each item must be when it arrives.

The night staff's wire still runs from INPUT straight to World Council. That is why the treaty arrived unlaminated.

## 4. Wire the order

A wire runs from an output jack (OUT) to an input jack (IN).

1. Tap INPUT's OUT. A wire hangs from it, and every jack that can take it blinks.
2. Tap LAMINATOR's IN. The wire plugs in. An output jack holds one wire, so the night staff's wire is gone.
3. Tap LAMINATOR's OUT, then World Council's IN.

Made a mistake? Press UNDO to take back the last step, or RESET to return to the night staff's wiring.

## 5. Run it

Press RUN. The board locks while the run plays. Each item leaves INPUT, gets laminated, and lands in its row at World Council. FAST speeds the run up; SKIP jumps to its end.

The teletype prints the mission report: "Signing ceremony: delivered as ordered." Under it, your score: the wires you used and the run's time. Most later orders set a par for one or both. Meet a par and its lamp lights on the order.

## 6. Go on

The report ends with SHARE and NEXT. Press NEXT. Order 2, Victory parade, opens.

Order 2 has two agents and no wiring yet. Read the strip, look at each agent's rule, and wire it yourself. Hint: think about the order in which the agents must see an item.

## What next

- ORDERS shows every order you can play, with its results found and its par lamps.
- MEDALS opens your commendations: three per act, 18 in all. The key shows how many you hold, as 0/18.
- Each act ends with a debrief and a save code. Keep the code to move your progress to another device.

For every key and lamp, see the [Console reference](reference-console.md). For tasks like zooming out or playing offline, see [Player tasks](how-to-player-tasks.md).
