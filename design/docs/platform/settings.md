# Settings

Design status: Defined.

## Goal

Settings: one setting, sound on or off. Serves no pillar; a platform need: [Settings](../system-map.md#platform).
Player decision: sound on or off. Feedback: the switch's lamp; the game's sounds play or stop.
Flavour: a toggle switch on the console, labelled AUDIO.
Not owned here: the sounds and when each plays, the feel pages; the save, [Saving](saving.md).

## Mechanics

### SETTING-01 Sound

The player flips the AUDIO switch, drawn as the run switch with a lamp as an agent's, on the board, the order list or the archive → every sound stops at once; flipped back, sounds play again from the next one.

- Its lamp: steady while sound is on, off while it is off.
- A browser with no setting kept starts with sound on.
- The setting is kept as [SAVE-01](saving.md#save-01-keep) says.
- The game sets no audio session type: its sounds play along with the player's other audio, as the browser's default allows.

Open: OQ-PLATFORM-05.

## Edge cases

- Sound off during the full alarm: the lamp lights, and no tone plays: [SETTING-01](#setting-01-sound), [VIEW-04](../board/run-view.md#view-04-full-alarm).
- A browser that keeps nothing: the setting lasts until the page closes: [SAVE-04](saving.md#save-04-no-keeping).
