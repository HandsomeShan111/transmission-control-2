Part of [Data](data.md#authored-lines): the lines of the RECORDS and COMMS families. Reports compose them: [REPORT-02](board/reports.md#report-02-item-lines).

## Authored lines, acts 1 and 2

Entries, one per agent and item kind, kinds in [Item kinds](data.md#item-kinds) order:

### LAMINATOR

- Address: LAMINATOR laminated the President's address to humanity.
- Treaty: LAMINATOR laminated the treaty, leaving no margin for amendment.
- Star chart: LAMINATOR laminated the star chart, which could no longer be folded.
- Confetti: LAMINATOR laminated the confetti one piece at a time.
- Cake: LAMINATOR pressed the cake between two sheets of clear film and sealed the edges.
- Coffee: LAMINATOR laminated the coffee, cup, lid and steam.
- Sandwich: LAMINATOR laminated the sandwich so the filling remained permanently visible.
- Satellite: LAMINATOR fed the satellite through its rollers, one solar panel at a time.
- Cube: LAMINATOR laminated each face of the cube, including the underside.
- Flag: LAMINATOR laminated the flag, which then stood up on its own.
- Moon rock: LAMINATOR laminated the moon rock in a pouch rated for documents only.
- Spacesuit: LAMINATOR laminated the empty spacesuit from collar to boots.

### SHREDDER

- Address: SHREDDER shredded the President's address to humanity into confetti.
- Treaty: SHREDDER shredded the treaty into confetti, clause by clause.
- Star chart: SHREDDER shredded the star chart into confetti, scattering the Great Bear across the tray.
- Confetti: SHREDDER noted that the confetti was already confetti and shredded it anyway.
- Cake: SHREDDER shredded the cake into confetti and logged the crumbs as confetti.
- Coffee: SHREDDER shredded the coffee into confetti by a method not recorded.
- Sandwich: SHREDDER shredded the sandwich into confetti, filling included.
- Satellite: SHREDDER shredded the satellite into confetti, antenna first.
- Cube: SHREDDER reduced the cube to confetti in a single pass.
- Flag: SHREDDER shredded the flag into confetti, starting at the fly end.
- Moon rock: SHREDDER shredded the moon rock into confetti and logged a request for new blades.
- Spacesuit: SHREDDER shredded the spacesuit into confetti with nobody inside.

- Star chart: INSPECTOR weighed the star chart in grams per square metre.
- Confetti: INSPECTOR inspected the confetti, piece by piece, for paper.
- Cake: INSPECTOR cut a thin section of the cake and examined it.
- Coffee: INSPECTOR dipped a test strip into the coffee.
- Sandwich: INSPECTOR opened the sandwich and examined each layer separately.
- Satellite: INSPECTOR circled the satellite twice with a magnifying glass.
- Cube: INSPECTOR rolled the cube across the bench and tapped each face.
- Flag: INSPECTOR examined the flag, hem first, for paper.
- Moon rock: INSPECTOR ran a probe along the moon rock, listening for a rustle.
- Spacesuit: INSPECTOR searched every pocket of the spacesuit for paper.
