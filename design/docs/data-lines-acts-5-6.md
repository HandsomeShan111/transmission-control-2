# Data, authored lines, acts 5 and 6

Design status: Defined.

Part of [Data](data.md#authored-lines): the lines of the AUDIT and DISPATCH families. Reports compose them: [REPORT-02](board/reports.md#report-02-item-lines).

## Authored lines, acts 5 and 6

Entries, one per agent and item kind, kinds in [Item kinds](data.md#item-kinds) order:

### STAMPER

- Address: STAMPER stamped the President's address to humanity APPROVED.
- Treaty: STAMPER stamped the treaty APPROVED, directly over the signatures.
- Star chart: STAMPER stamped the star chart APPROVED, once per hemisphere.
- Confetti: STAMPER stamped every piece of the confetti APPROVED, skipping none.
- Cake: STAMPER stamped the cake APPROVED, and the ink soaked into the sponge.
- Coffee: STAMPER stamped the coffee APPROVED, and the impression lasted two seconds.
- Sandwich: STAMPER stamped the sandwich APPROVED, top slice only, in food-safe ink.
- Satellite: STAMPER stamped the satellite APPROVED on the side facing Earth.
- Cube: STAMPER stamped the cube APPROVED on whichever face was upward.
- Flag: STAMPER stamped the flag APPROVED in the lower corner, as regulations required.
- Moon rock: STAMPER stamped the moon rock APPROVED on its flattest side.
- Spacesuit: STAMPER stamped the spacesuit APPROVED and noted the contents as none.

### SEALER

- Address: SEALER sealed the President's address to humanity in wax.
- Treaty: SEALER sealed the treaty with a wax seal the size of a saucer.
- Star chart: SEALER rolled the star chart, tied it and sealed the knot in wax.
- Confetti: SEALER sealed the confetti in wax, using one seal for the whole heap.
- Cake: SEALER sealed the cake in a uniform coat of red wax.
- Coffee: SEALER poured wax over the coffee until the cup was sealed.
- Sandwich: SEALER sealed the sandwich in wax, and the wax set around the lettuce.
- Satellite: SEALER sealed the satellite in wax and pressed one seal on the antenna.
- Cube: SEALER sealed the cube in wax, a seal at each of the eight corners.
- Flag: SEALER folded the flag into thirds and sealed the folds in wax.
- Moon rock: SEALER sealed the moon rock in wax, preserving it against conditions on Earth.
- Spacesuit: SEALER sealed the empty spacesuit in wax at the neck, wrists and ankles.

### TALLY

- Address: TALLY counted the marks on the President's address to humanity.
- Treaty: TALLY checked the treaty for marks and counted them twice.
- Star chart: TALLY entered each mark on the star chart into its ledger.
- Confetti: TALLY counted the confetti's marks and ignored the individual pieces.
- Cake: TALLY tallied the marks on the cake and ignored the crumbs.
- Coffee: TALLY counted the marks on the coffee, cup rim included.
- Sandwich: TALLY added up the marks on the sandwich, top slice first.
- Satellite: TALLY counted the satellite's marks and transmitted the figure to ground.
- Cube: TALLY totalled the marks on the cube and entered the sum in triplicate.
- Flag: TALLY counted the marks on the flag, working left to right.
- Moon rock: TALLY counted the marks on the moon rock, craters excluded.
- Spacesuit: TALLY counted the marks on the spacesuit, front and back.

### WRAPPER

- Address: WRAPPER wrapped the President's address to humanity in flight tape.
- Treaty: WRAPPER wrapped the treaty for flight, taping each page separately.
- Star chart: WRAPPER wrapped the star chart for flight and marked the parcel THIS WAY UP.
- Confetti: WRAPPER wrapped the confetti for flight and used the whole roll of tape.
- Cake: WRAPPER wrapped the cake for flight and labelled it FRAGILE.
- Coffee: WRAPPER taped the lid of the coffee and wrapped the cup for flight.
- Sandwich: WRAPPER wrapped the sandwich for flight and labelled it PERISHABLE.
- Satellite: WRAPPER wrapped the satellite for flight in forty metres of tape.
- Cube: WRAPPER wrapped the cube for flight, taping all twelve edges.
- Flag: WRAPPER wrapped the flag for flight until no design remained visible.
- Moon rock: WRAPPER wrapped the moon rock for flight and labelled it FROM THE MOON.
- Spacesuit: WRAPPER wrapped the spacesuit for flight, sleeves folded across its empty chest.

### LABELLER

- Address: LABELLER labelled the President's address to humanity URGENT.
- Treaty: LABELLER labelled the treaty URGENT in letters two centimetres high.
- Star chart: LABELLER labelled the star chart URGENT, covering the North Star.
- Confetti: LABELLER attached a label reading URGENT to the confetti, larger than any piece.
- Cake: LABELLER labelled the cake URGENT, the label pressed into the top layer.
- Coffee: LABELLER labelled the coffee URGENT, the label applied just below the lid.
- Sandwich: LABELLER labelled the sandwich URGENT, giving the lettuce priority over the bread.
- Satellite: LABELLER labelled the satellite URGENT in letters legible from the ground.
- Cube: LABELLER labelled the cube URGENT at the exact centre of the top face.
- Flag: LABELLER labelled the flag URGENT, sewing the label to the hoist edge.
- Moon rock: LABELLER labelled the moon rock URGENT, noting that it had waited four billion years.
- Spacesuit: LABELLER labelled the spacesuit URGENT, covering the name tag, which was blank.

### VERIFIER

- Address: VERIFIER compared the President's address to humanity against the order.
- Treaty: VERIFIER read the order and the treaty side by side.
- Star chart: VERIFIER held the star chart up to the order, star by star.
- Confetti: VERIFIER measured the confetti against the order with a ruler.
- Cake: VERIFIER checked the cake against the order, ingredient by ingredient.
- Coffee: VERIFIER checked the coffee against the order and took its temperature.
- Sandwich: VERIFIER opened the sandwich and checked each layer against the order.
- Satellite: VERIFIER consulted the order, then the satellite, then the order again.
- Cube: VERIFIER checked the cube against the order with a set square.
- Flag: VERIFIER unfurled the flag beside the order and compared the two.
- Moon rock: VERIFIER weighed the moon rock and compared the weight with the order.
- Spacesuit: VERIFIER checked the spacesuit against the order and looked inside.
